How a row is valued

implied = 103.0247 × TVL0.5223 × Peers × Momentum × Record × Kin

The size curve is an OLS fit on log10 market cap against log10 TVL over the snapshot’s 128 traded tokens (R² 0.19). The curve is honest about what it does not explain: most of a token’s launch value is not its TVL. The factors bend the curve, they do not replace it.

Peers
Each category’s mean log residual, shrunk toward zero with 20 pseudo-peers. Liquid Staking pays ×0.74, Prediction Market ×1.37. A category with no traded comps gets no offset.
Momentum
1 + half the 7-day TVL change, clamped to [0.6, 1.4]. A protocol growing 20% in a week is worth 10% more; one shrinking 20% is worth 10% less. No change recorded counts as no change.
Record
0.70 rising linearly to 1.00 over 730 days running with no token. A protocol listed two years ago with no token has survived two years of chances to launch one. An unknown listing date counts as 365 days.
Kin
×0.5 when a sibling under the same name stem already trades. No board row hits it.
Breadth
Chain count was tested and is not in the model: adding it did not improve the out-of-sample miss, so it stays out rather than flattering the fit.

The farm, priced

Deposit $1,000, hold 60 days. The slice is 10% of the implied launch, pro rata: 0.10 × implied × $1,000 / TVL. Costs are gas for 4 transactions on the protocol’s cheapest chain plus $6.58 — what a 4% T-bill pays on $1,000 over 60 days. FARM when the net clears $5 an hour of the 2 active hours the plan takes; THIN when the net is non-negative but below that; SKIP when the net is negative. Break-even odds are costs divided by the slice: the launch chance at which the plan pays for itself.

Out of sample

Leave-one-out over the 128 comps: median miss ×3.52, within 3× 46%, within 10× 77%, rank correlation 0.40. A flat multiple (median market-cap-to-TVL, no curve) misses by ×5.07. The curve wins on level, not on ranking — the flat multiple ranks marginally better (0.42). Time split: fit on the 44 comps listed before 2024, test on the 51 newer ones — median miss ×2.94 against ×5.20 flat. Bootstrap, 200 rounds, seed 20261114: a = 3.018 ± 0.643, b = 0.522 ± 0.081. Each row’s P25–P75 band is the implied value times the leave-one-out residual band, ×0.36 to ×4.70.

What this is not

The implied value is this radar’s own claim, fitted from one snapshot — not a forecast, not a price, and not a promise that a token will ever launch. Most pre-token protocols never launch one. Real airdrops never ask for a seed phrase, a private key, or money up front.
