How a row is valued

implied = 103.9578 × TVL0.3940 × Peers × Momentum × Record

The size curve is an OLS fit on log10 market cap against log10 TVL over the 92 traded tokens that pass the board’s eligibility rule — one per ticker, no CEX, no bridge, no liquid-staking product (R² 0.10). The curve is fitted on the population it ranks. Most of a token’s launch value is not its TVL; the factors bend the curve, they do not replace it.

Peers
Each category’s mean log residual, shrunk toward zero with 40 pseudo-peers. Liquid Staking pays ×0.84, Basis Trading ×1.18. A category with no traded comps gets no offset. The shrinkage was tested at 4, 8, 12, 20 and 40: 40 wins the time split, 8 and 4 lose on both validations.
Momentum
1 + a tenth of the 7-day TVL change, clamped to [0.6, 1.4]. A protocol growing 20% in a week is worth 2% more; one shrinking 20% is worth 2% less. No change recorded counts as no change.
Record
0.85 rising linearly to 1.00 over 730 days running with no token. An unknown listing date counts as 365 days.
Kin
Tested and not in the model: no pre-token protocol in the snapshot shares a name stem with a traded token (0 of 342), so the factor never fires and stays out rather than flattering the formula.
Breadth
Chain count was tested and is not in the model: adding it did not improve the out-of-sample miss, so it stays out rather than flattering the fit. A median slope and a Huber fit were tested too and did not beat OLS out of sample.
Quadratic curve
A squared log-TVL term: the bootstrap cannot sign its coefficient (−0.09 ± 0.12, positive 22% of rounds) and the time split gets worse (×2.15). Out.
Interactions
A TVL × momentum interaction: LOO ×3.29, time split ×3.09 — worse on both. Out.
Change as a regressor
The 7-day change as a regressor instead of a factor: LOO ×3.12, time split ×2.31 — the ranking falls and the time split is worse. Out.
Log-age
Log days-since-listing as a regressor, replacing the record factor: LOO ×3.15, time split ×5.76 — the time split collapses. Out.
Five-feature regression
A five-feature regression (quadratic, log-age, change, chains): LOO ×3.71, time split ×7.94 — overfit. Out.

The farm, priced

Deposit $1,000, hold 60 days. The slice is 10% of the implied launch, pro rata: 0.10 × implied × $1,000 / TVL. Costs are gas for 4 transactions on the protocol’s cheapest chain plus $6.58 — what a 4% T-bill pays on $1,000 over 60 days. FARM only when the net at half the implied value — the launch coming in at half what the curve says — still clears $10; THIN when the point-estimate net is non-negative but below that bar; SKIP when the net is negative. The old bar ($5 an hour on the point estimate) let 141 of 198 rows through; the new one lets 71 through, and the 70 it drops are rows whose farm dies on a single haircut. Every plan carries calendar dates: deposit 14 Nov 2026, a check each Saturday, withdrawal 13 Jan 2027. Break-even odds are costs divided by the slice: the launch chance at which the plan pays for itself. The bridge that moves the $1,000 onto the cheapest chain costs about $0 on Ethereum, $1 on Solana, Polygon or BSC, $2 on Avalanche and $3 on Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, zkSync or Linea — priced beside each plan, not inside it, because where the money starts is the reader’s to know. Each row’s detail shows the net after the bridge, and the net if the launch comes in at half or twice the implied value.

Out of sample

Leave-one-out over the 92 comps the curve is fitted on: median miss ×3.26, within 3× 49%, rank correlation 0.33. A flat multiple (median market-cap-to-TVL, no curve) misses by ×4.65; on ranking the curve still trails it (0.34). Time split: fit on the 29 comps listed before 2024, test on the 41 newer ones — median miss ×1.90 against ×4.52 flat, rank correlation 0.31 against 0.31 flat. On all 128 traded tokens the same fit reads ×3.59 leave-one-out, within 3× 45%, rank 0.41, against ×4.78 flat (rank 0.43), and ×2.45 time split (fit 29, test 51) against ×4.61 flat, rank 0.34 against 0.35 — worse, because the curve no longer fits the CEX and bridge tokens it does not rank. Walk-forward, fitting on tokens listed before each year and testing on the next year’s: 2023 ×3.00 (13 tokens), 2024 ×1.99 (12), pooled ×2.52 against ×4.61 flat. Every row’s rank over the same 200 bootstrap refits is in its detail view. Bootstrap, 200 rounds, seed 20261114: a = 4.00 ± 0.94, b = 0.39 ± 0.12. Each row’s P25–P75 band is the implied value times the leave-one-out residual band, ×0.34 to ×4.83. By category it misses least on CDP (×1.07) and RWA (×1.67); its worst miss is Rain, worth ×773 the curve — the snapshot’s own outlier, kept in the fit rather than cut out.

What this is not

The implied value is this radar’s own claim, fitted from one snapshot — not a forecast, not a price, and not a promise that a token will ever launch. Most pre-token protocols never launch one. Real airdrops never ask for a seed phrase, a private key, or money up front.
